This text-based adventure project is a modular Python application that simulates the historical 1889 San Miguel Valley Bank robbery. The game features two distinct paths—executing the heist as an outlaw or defending the accused in court—driven by player choices, external file-based inventory management, and dynamic narrative pacing.   Upon startup, the application captures the user's name and calculates their generational connection to the 1889 setting by utilizing their birth year. A core branching decision—whether to attend school—routes the player into either the Lawyer or Robber storyline. Instead of using internal volatile memory, the system generates a persistent external text file to track the player's inventory, automatically populating it with 30 path-specific items such as a "Stick of Dynamite" or a "Sloppy Poker Ledger".   Players choosing the outlaw path must select a tactical approach: Stealth, Force, Trickery, or Gentleman. Success depends on sequentially overcoming three obstacles—the entry, the vault, and the escape—by parsing their inventory and inputting the exact tool required for their chosen strategy. The system uses a retry mechanic that awards partial points for delayed reactions, alongside a string-indexing puzzle for the vault's lock. Final scores dictate the ending, ranging from an immediate capture or fatal shootout to a historic recreation of Butch Cassidy's actual horse-relay escape.   Players acting as defense attorneys must select a strategic alibi for Butch Cassidy, such as Mistaken Identity or a Timeline Defense, or accept a plea deal. During cross-examination, players present specific evidence to three witnesses to counter their claims. Submitting tactically incorrect evidence triggers contextual failures, while offering useless flavor items prompts judicial reprimands. Scoring sufficient points secures a Not Guilty verdict, while lower scores force a high-stakes closing argument to salvage the trial.
